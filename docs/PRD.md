Product Vision

Pravaas is a digital traveler identity and hospitality platform that enables seamless hotel check-in, booking management, traveler verification, and statutory compliance through a single traveler profile and QR-based journey.

The long-term vision is to become the hospitality equivalent of DigiYatra.
